Privacy Policy

A Thousand Dreams (iOS) · by Khun Tech · Effective date: 31 October 2026

A Thousand Dreams ("the app") is a dream dictionary and Thai Government Lottery results app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Camera

The app asks for camera access only so you can scan the code on the front of your lottery ticket. The camera image is processed on your device to read the code and is never saved or sent anywhere. You can turn camera access off at any time in iOS Settings and still type the ticket number by hand.

Internet connection

To show lottery results and dream-card pictures, the app downloads public files from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), operated by GitHub, Inc. Like any website, GitHub may receive technical information such as your IP address when these files are downloaded. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Data stored on your device

Downloaded results and pictures are kept in the app's cache on your device so they load faster. This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if advertising is added in a future version), the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
